# Product Marketing Context

## Brand 1: Shifa Mehendi Nails Instant Cone
 **Target Audience:** Busy women, students, and festive shoppers in Mumbai looking for quick, traditional aesthetic solutions.
- **Product Benefits:** 10-minute application, reusable, traditional mehendi patterns, no-mess alternative to cone mehendi.
- **Brand Voice:** Vibrant, trendy, and time-saving.

## Brand 2: Ayisha Bridal Mehendi Cone
- **Target Audience:** Brides-to-be and their families in Mumbai/Maharashtra looking for premium, intricate bridal artistry.
- **Product Benefits:** Professional on-site service, organic long-lasting stain, custom bridal storytelling through designs.
- **Brand Voice:** Elegant, heritage-focused, and trustworthy.
